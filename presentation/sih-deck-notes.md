# Smart India Hackathon Presentation Notes: RailDrishti AI

## Problem Statement
Indian Railways handles over 24 million passengers daily across 13,000+ passenger trains. Existing ETA systems rely primarily on static scheduled timetables or simplistic distance-over-average-speed estimations that fail during dynamic disruptions (weather, loop-line precedence, speed restrictions, section congestion).

## Our Solution: RailDrishti AI
- **Explainable Dynamic ETA:** Predicts station ETAs using live physics simulation, sectional congestion, TSRs (Temporary Speed Restrictions), and gradient models.
- **Root-Cause Explainability:** Explains the exact breakdown behind every delay in passenger-friendly and controller-actionable terms.
- **Single Pane of Glass:** Specialized dashboards for Passengers, Controllers, Station Masters, and Operations Analysts.

## Prototype Disclaimer
*Prototype mode — using simulated railway operational data. Production integration requires authorized Railway feeds.*
